Health Information Disclaimer

Please read this before relying on anything on this website.

Last updated 25 September 2026

General information only

The content on Quiet Mind Sleep is provided for general information and self-help purposes. It is not medical advice, and it is not intended to be a substitute for professional diagnosis, advice or treatment from a GP, pharmacist or other qualified health professional.

Quiet Mind Sleep is not a medical provider. We do not have clinicians on staff and we do not review your individual circumstances.

Please do not

  • rely on this website to diagnose a sleep or health problem
  • delay seeking medical advice because of something you have read here
  • stop, start or change any medication on the basis of our content. Always talk to the professional who prescribed it.

When to seek help

Speak to a GP if sleep problems persist for several weeks, if they affect your daytime functioning or mood, if you snore loudly with pauses in breathing, or if you have any other concern about your health. Our guide on when to see a GP about sleep explains what to expect.

If you feel unable to keep yourself safe, call 999 or go to A&E. Samaritans are available 24 hours a day on 116 123. NHS 111 can help when it is urgent but not an emergency.

Accuracy

We work hard to keep our guides accurate and current, following our editorial policy, but medical understanding changes and we cannot guarantee that every page is complete or up to date. If you think something is wrong, please tell us via our corrections process.

Products

Product information is drawn from manufacturer descriptions and general knowledge of the product type. Suitability varies between individuals. Follow the manufacturer's safety instructions, and seek advice if you have a condition that may be affected (for example, weighted blankets are not suitable for young children or for some people with respiratory or mobility conditions).
