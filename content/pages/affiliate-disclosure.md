## The short version

Quiet Mind Sleep is free to read. To cover the costs of running the site, some of the links to products are affiliate links. If you click one and go on to buy something, the retailer may pay us a small commission. **You pay nothing extra.**

## Amazon Associates

Quiet Mind Sleep is a participant in the Amazon EU Associates Programme, an affiliate advertising programme designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.co.uk.

**As an Amazon Associate, Quiet Mind Sleep earns from qualifying purchases.**

## Other programmes

We may join other affiliate programmes with sleep, bedding and home retailers in future. Any such link will follow the same rules described here, and this page will be updated.

## How you can tell

- Product recommendation boxes and comparison tables are clearly labelled, and their buttons go to a retailer's site.
- Pages that contain affiliate links carry a short notice near the recommendations and at the end of the article.
- Affiliate links open in a new tab and are marked in the page code so that search engines treat them as sponsored.

## What affiliate links never change

- **Which products we mention.** Products are chosen because a type of product genuinely helps with a problem described in the guide, such as light, noise or temperature. Many of our guides contain no products at all.
- **What we say about them.** We use factual, manufacturer-style descriptions and explain limitations as well as benefits. We do not write fake reviews or claim testing we have not done.
- **Prices and availability.** We do not display prices or stock information, because they change. Always check the retailer's page.

## Your choice

You are never obliged to use our links. If you would rather not, you can search for any product directly. The guides will be just as useful either way.
