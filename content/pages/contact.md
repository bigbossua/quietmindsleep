## Before you write

**We cannot give medical advice.** Quiet Mind Sleep is an information website, not a clinical service, and we are unable to answer questions about your individual sleep, health or medication. If you are worried about your sleep, please speak to a GP or pharmacist. Our guide on [when to see a GP about sleep](@sleep-questions/when-to-see-a-gp-about-sleep) may help you decide.

**If you need urgent help.** If you feel you cannot keep yourself safe, call 999 or go to A&E. If you need someone to talk to, Samaritans are available 24 hours a day on 116 123 (free from any phone), or you can contact NHS 111.

## Working with us

We do not accept sponsored posts, paid placements or guest articles, and we do not sell links. If you represent a sleep product or service and think it is relevant to our readers, you are welcome to email us; we will assess it on the same editorial basis as everything else on the site.
