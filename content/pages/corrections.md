## Reporting an error

Email us via the [contact page](@contact) with:

- the link to the page
- the sentence or section concerned
- what you believe is wrong and, if possible, a source

We aim to acknowledge reports within a few working days.

## What we do

- **Factual errors** are corrected as soon as they are verified. Where the error was material, a note is added to the guide stating what changed and when.
- **Clarifications** (where wording was accurate but could mislead) are made without a note unless the change is significant.
- **Out-of-date advice** is updated when reputable guidance changes, and the "updated" date on the guide is refreshed.
- **Product changes** (a product type no longer available or no longer suitable) lead to the recommendation being removed or replaced, never silently swapped for something unrelated.

## What we will not do

We will not remove or alter a guide simply because a company, brand or individual dislikes it, and we do not accept payment to change editorial content.
